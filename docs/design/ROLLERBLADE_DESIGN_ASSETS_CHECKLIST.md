### 🎨 Design Style Guidelines

#### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Pink (#ff2d72, #ff4b86, #ff6b9d)
- **Background:** Light pink to white gradient
- **Text:** Dark gray (#111827) on light, white on dark
- **Accents:** 
  - Red (#ef4444) for PROMO
  - Blue (#3b82f6) for GROUP
  - Green (#22c55e) for NEW

#### Photography Style
- **Lighting:** Bright, well-lit, energetic
- **Composition:** Dynamic angles, not static
- **Subjects:** Diverse age groups, families, friends
- **Mood:** Fun, energetic, safe, professional
- **Arena branding:** SparkStage logos visible when possible

#### Image Optimization
- Use 70-80% JPG quality for balance
- Or use WebP format for better compression
- Avoid motion blur (unless intentional)
- Ensure faces are visible and happy
- Good depth of field (subject in focus)

### 💡 Tips for Photographers

1. **Shoot in RAW** - Easier to edit and optimize later
2. **Multiple angles** - Provide variations for each shot
3. **Wider framing** - Allow for cropping flexibility
4. **Model releases** - Get consent from visible faces
5. **Arena permission** - Coordinate shooting schedule
6. **Natural expressions** - Candid shots work better than posed
7. **Safety visible** - Show helmets, pads being used
8. **Diverse subjects** - Various ages, genders, ethnicities
9. **Golden hour** - Best natural lighting for indoor arena
10. **Action shots** - Capture movement and energy
